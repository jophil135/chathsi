# CHATHSI Advertising System

- Advertisers submit a business name, headline, description, HTTPS destination, optional image/video, campaign dates, placement and budget.
- Campaigns start as `pending`; they never appear in the feed until an administrator approves both campaign and payment.
- Admin review is recorded in the audit log. Admins can approve or reject a campaign and payment together.
- Approved feed ads are labelled **Sponsored** and can be reported by members.
- Impression and click counters are recorded. No sensitive-interest targeting or user profiling is implemented.
- Campaign budget is a declared campaign budget, not an automatically charged or metered balance. No payment gateway or automated billing is connected. The administrator must verify payment externally before using the approval control.
- Advertisements must be appropriate for a teen audience. Gambling, alcohol, nicotine, drugs, weapons and adult content are prohibited.
- All destinations must use HTTPS.
